# Campfire safety basics (pipeline test document)

This file is tracked in git on purpose: it lets you test the whole RAG pipeline
without downloading any real documents. Build the index with only this file in
`docs/`, then ask the agent "How do I put out a campfire safely?" — the answer
should mention pouring water, stirring the ashes, and pouring again.

Always build your campfire at least three meters away from tents, trees and dry brush. Clear the ground down to bare soil in a circle of about one meter around the fire pit. Keep a container of water or a shovel with sand within reach before you light anything.

When cooking over a campfire, let the flames die down to glowing embers first. Embers give steady, even heat, while open flames burn food on the outside and leave it raw inside. A simple trick for baking bread on a stick: hold the dough high above the embers and rotate it slowly for about ten minutes.

To put a campfire out completely, pour water over it, stir the ashes with a stick, and pour again. The ashes should be cold to the touch before you leave the site. Never bury a fire under sand or soil, because the embers can smolder underground for hours and reignite later.
